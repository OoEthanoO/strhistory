# Grade 10 note image sources

Inventory for the images in the Grade 10 notes on Canada and the First World War
(`src/content/topics/canada-*.mdx`), checked on 12 October 2026. The images were
supplied as screenshots with the notes. STR History converted them to JPEG (at
most 1,400 pixels wide) and has not cropped, retouched, recoloured or generated
any image content.

Captions say what each image is and who made it, and describe a later painting
as a later depiction. Where a photograph's source is not yet recorded, its caption
describes only what it shows, without claiming a place, date or unit.

## Identified works

### The Empire Needs Men! (1915)

- File: [empire-needs-men-1915.jpg](./empire-needs-men-1915.jpg), in `canada-declares-war-1914`.
- Work: recruiting poster by Arthur Wardle, published by the Parliamentary
  Recruiting Committee, London (poster no. 58, printed on the poster), 1915.
- Reuse: public domain. [Original image and rights record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Empire_Needs_Men_WWI.jpg).

### The Quebec Chronicle, 5 August 1914

- File: [quebec-chronicle-1914-08-05.jpg](./quebec-chronicle-1914-08-05.jpg), in `canada-declares-war-1914`.
- Work: front page of *The Quebec Chronicle*, Quebec, Wednesday 5 August 1914;
  the masthead shows the title and date.
- Reuse: public domain (published 1914). Source page not yet recorded.

### Statesmen of World War I

- File: [statesmen-of-world-war-i.jpg](./statesmen-of-world-war-i.jpg), in `canada-declares-war-1914`.
- Work: Sir James Guthrie, oil on canvas, 1924–1930, National Portrait Gallery,
  London (NPG 2463). The sitters include Sir Robert Borden, Prime Minister of
  Canada.
- Reuse: public domain. [Original image and rights record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Statesmen_of_World_War_I_by_Sir_James_Guthrie.jpg).

### The Canadians at Ypres

- File: [canadians-at-ypres-wollen.jpg](./canadians-at-ypres-wollen.jpg), in `canada-ypres-1915`.
- Work: William Barnes Wollen (1857–1936), 1915, regimental museum of Princess
  Patricia's Canadian Light Infantry, Calgary. It depicts the regiment at
  Bellewaarde Ridge on 8 May 1915, during the Second Battle of Ypres.
- Reuse: public domain (the artist died in 1936). [Original image and rights record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Canadians_at_Ypres_by_William_Barnes_Wollen.jpg).

### Gassed (detail)

- File: [gassed-sargent-detail.jpg](./gassed-sargent-detail.jpg), in `canada-ypres-1915`.
- Work: John Singer Sargent, *Gassed*, completed 1919, Imperial War Museum,
  London. It depicts soldiers blinded by mustard gas at Le Bac-du-Sud, between
  Arras and Doullens, in August 1918.
- Reuse: public domain. [Original image and rights record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:John_Singer_Sargent_-_Gassed_Detail_1.jpg).

## Photographs whose source is not yet recorded

These were supplied without source details, and a search of Wikimedia Commons
and the related Wikipedia articles by image similarity found no match. Record
each one's source page, photographer, date, place and reuse status here before
adding detail to its caption.

- [soldiers-box-respirators.jpg](./soldiers-box-respirators.jpg), in `canada-ypres-1915`:
  soldiers in steel helmets and box respirators climbing out of a trench through
  gas or smoke. The equipment dates it to 1916 or later, not April 1915.
- [soldiers-muddy-road.jpg](./soldiers-muddy-road.jpg), in `canada-somme-1916`:
  soldiers in steel helmets and fleece coats beside pack horses on a muddy road.
  Supplied as "Canadian troops making their way to the front".
- [soldiers-fixed-bayonets.jpg](./soldiers-fixed-bayonets.jpg), in `canada-somme-1916`:
  soldiers with fixed bayonets in a trench. Supplied as "Canadian troops
  preparing to go over the top".
- [shattered-wood.jpg](./shattered-wood.jpg), in `canada-somme-1916`: a wood
  destroyed by shellfire. Supplied as "Beaumont Hamel where the Newfoundland
  Regiment fell".
- [shell-case-dump.jpg](./shell-case-dump.jpg), in `canada-somme-1916`: a dump
  of empty artillery shell cases. Supplied as a Somme photograph.
- [cavalry-charge.jpg](./cavalry-charge.jpg), in `canada-somme-1916`: cavalry
  charging with drawn swords.

## Not used

- A photograph supplied as "German troops releasing chlorine gas canisters across
  Allied positions" was left out. It shows two men without gas masks tending
  smoking pots in an open field, unlike the cylinders dug into the German trenches
  at Ypres on 22 April 1915, and its source was not recorded.
- Two images that no note used, a soldier making grave crosses and a map of the
  British plan for 1 July 1916 on the Somme, were not added.
