---
year: 1945
title: The end of the Second World War
summary: Germany and Japan are defeated and occupied, the United Nations is founded, and the wartime alliance begins to break down into the Cold War.
highlights:
  - Germany surrendered in May 1945 and was divided into four occupation zones.
  - The atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki preceded Japan's surrender in August–September 1945.
  - The United Nations Charter was signed in June 1945.
