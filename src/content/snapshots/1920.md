---
year: 1920
title: The peace settlements
summary: The German, Austro-Hungarian and Russian empires have fallen and the Ottoman Empire is being partitioned; new states emerge in central and eastern Europe, the League of Nations is founded, and Russia is in civil war.
highlights:
  - The Treaty of Versailles (1919) and related treaties redrew the map of Europe.
  - Poland, Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia and the Baltic states became independent.
  - Former Ottoman and German territories became League of Nations mandates.
