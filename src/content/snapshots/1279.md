---
year: 1279
title: The Mongol world
summary: Kublai Khan completes the conquest of the Song, and the Mongol empire — now divided into four khanates — stretches from Korea to the borders of Hungary.
highlights:
  - The Yuan dynasty ruled all of China after the fall of the Southern Song in 1279.
  - The Golden Horde, Ilkhanate and Chagatai Khanate governed the western Mongol lands.
  - The Mamluks of Egypt had halted the Mongol advance at Ain Jalut in 1260.
