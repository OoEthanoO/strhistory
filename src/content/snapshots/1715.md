year: 1715
title: After Utrecht
summary: The Peace of Utrecht ends the War of the Spanish Succession, Louis XIV dies, and Peter the Great's Russia is rising in the Great Northern War.
highlights:
  - The Treaties of Utrecht (1713) confirmed a Bourbon king in Spain but kept the crowns of France and Spain apart.
  - Great Britain gained Gibraltar, Minorca and Newfoundland.
  - The Qing dynasty under the Kangxi Emperor ruled China.
