---
term: Battalion
when: 1914–1918
definition: A unit of about 1,000 soldiers at full strength. A regiment could have a single battalion or dozens of them.
significance: Battalions were the building blocks of the First World War’s armies. At Beaumont-Hamel on 1 July 1916, the Newfoundland Regiment (a single battalion) sent 801 men into the attack and suffered about 700 casualties in minutes, showing how quickly a unit could be destroyed in trench warfare.
