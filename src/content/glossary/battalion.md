---
term: Battalion
when: 1914–1918
definition: A military unit of approximately 800–1,000 men at full strength, the basic tactical unit of infantry in the First World War.
significance: Battalions were the building blocks of the First World War's armies. At Beaumont-Hamel on 1 July 1916, the Newfoundland Regiment (a single battalion of 801 men) suffered 700 casualties in minutes — illustrating how quickly a unit could be destroyed in trench warfare.
