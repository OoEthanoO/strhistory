---
term: Lytton Commission
when: 1932–1933
definition: The League of Nations inquiry, led by Britain's Lord Lytton, into the conflict in Manchuria. Its report rejected Japan's claim of self-defence and recommended autonomy for Manchuria under Chinese sovereignty.
significance: When the League adopted the report in February 1933, Japan walked out and left the League. The League had judged Japan but would not act — exposing the weakness of collective security.
