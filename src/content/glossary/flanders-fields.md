---
term: Flanders Fields
when: 1915
definition: "The name Flanders Fields comes from the geographical fields upon which this battle was fought. Flanders was the historic name for Belgium, and since Ypres was in Belgium, hence the name “Flanders Fields”."
significance: Written by Canadian Lieutenant-Colonel John McCrae after the Second Battle of Ypres, the poem of the same name transformed Flanders into an enduring symbol of wartime sacrifice and established the red poppy as the universal Commonwealth emblem of remembrance.
