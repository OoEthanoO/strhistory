---
term: Flanders Fields
when: 1915
definition: The fields of Flanders, a historic region now mostly in Belgium, with smaller parts in France and the Netherlands. Ypres lies in Belgian Flanders, so the battlefields around it became known as Flanders fields.
significance: Canadian doctor John McCrae, later a lieutenant-colonel, wrote the poem “In Flanders Fields” during the Second Battle of Ypres in May 1915. It transformed Flanders into an enduring symbol of wartime sacrifice and helped make the red poppy the Commonwealth’s emblem of remembrance.
