---
term: Dominion
when: 1867–1931
definition: A semi-autonomous state in the British Empire under the British Crown, with an independent domestic policy and no control over foreign policy.
significance: Dominion status meant that when Britain declared war in 1914, Canada was automatically at war — it had no legal power to remain neutral or to conduct independent diplomacy. The experience of the First World War drove Dominions to demand full equality, achieved through the Statute of Westminster (1931).
