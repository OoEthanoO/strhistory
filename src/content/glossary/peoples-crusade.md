---
term: People's Crusade
when: 1096
definition: A large, poorly organised wave of crusaders, many of them non-combatants, led by preachers such as Peter the Hermit, who set out before the main armies.
significance: It was destroyed by the Seljuks in Anatolia in October 1096. It shows how Urban's call spread beyond the knightly class he intended — and how uncontrollable popular enthusiasm could be.
