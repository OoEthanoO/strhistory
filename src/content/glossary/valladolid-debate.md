---
term: Valladolid debate
when: 1550–1551
definition: A formal debate in Spain between Bartolomé de las Casas, who defended the full humanity and rights of Indigenous peoples, and Juan Ginés de Sepúlveda, who argued that conquest was justified.
significance: It was one of the first European debates about the rights of colonised peoples. It produced no clear verdict, but shaped later arguments about empire and human rights.
---
