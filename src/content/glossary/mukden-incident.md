---
term: Mukden Incident
when: 18 September 1931
definition: A small explosion on the Japanese-owned South Manchuria Railway near Mukden, staged by Kwantung Army officers and blamed on Chinese soldiers.
significance: It gave the Kwantung Army a pretext to occupy all of Manchuria. It is the starting point of the IB case study on Japanese expansion and a clear example of military insubordination shaping policy.
