---
term: Newfoundland Regiment
when: 1914–1918
definition: The principal infantry regiment raised by Newfoundland — then a separate Dominion, not yet part of Canada — to serve in the First World War.
significance: On 1 July 1916, the first day of the Somme, 801 soldiers of the Newfoundland Regiment attacked German positions at Beaumont-Hamel. Of these, 700 became casualties and only 68 answered roll call the next morning. It was the most devastating single-day loss in Newfoundland history, and the site is now a memorial park.
