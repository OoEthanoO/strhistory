---
term: Malintzin (La Malinche)
when: 1519–1520s
definition: A Nahua woman, also called Doña Marina, who became Cortés's interpreter, adviser and mistress.
significance: Her knowledge of Nahuatl, Maya and Mesoamerican politics made negotiation and alliance-building possible. In Mexican memory she has been seen both as a traitor and as a survivor with little choice.
