---
term: Tank
when: 1916–present
definition: An armoured, tracked military vehicle able to move across broken ground and absorb small-arms fire, used to cross enemy trenches and break defensive lines.
significance: Tanks were used in battle for the first time at the Somme on 15 September 1916. Early models were unreliable, but the concept of mobile armoured firepower was proven. By Vimy Ridge and the final Allied offensives of 1918, tanks were a decisive element of modern combined-arms warfare.
