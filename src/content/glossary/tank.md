---
term: Tank
when: 1916–present
definition: An armoured, tracked fighting vehicle, designed to resist rifle and machine-gun fire while crossing trenches and barbed wire.
significance: Tanks were first used in battle on 15 September 1916, at Flers–Courcelette during the Somme offensive. Early models were slow and unreliable, and the few at Vimy Ridge in 1917 achieved little in the mud. By the Allied offensives of 1918, such as Amiens, massed tanks had become an important part of combined-arms warfare.
