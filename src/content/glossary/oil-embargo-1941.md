---
term: US oil embargo
when: July–August 1941
definition: The freezing of Japanese assets in the United States and the halt of oil exports to Japan after Japan occupied southern French Indochina; Britain and the Netherlands followed suit.
significance: Japan imported most of its oil from the US. The embargo forced its leaders to choose between retreating from China or seizing the oil of the Dutch East Indies — the decision that led to Pearl Harbor.
