---
term: Crusader states
when: 1098–1291
definition: The four Latin Christian states created in the Levant after the First Crusade — the County of Edessa, the Principality of Antioch, the Kingdom of Jerusalem and the County of Tripoli.
significance: They depended on a steady flow of reinforcements from Europe and on divisions among their Muslim neighbours. Their gradual loss, from Edessa in 1144 to Acre in 1291, drove further crusades.
