---
term: Canadian Corps
when: 1915–1919
definition: The formation of all four Canadian divisions fighting as a unified national army on the Western Front, established in 1915 and given independent command under Canadian officers by 1917.
significance: The Canadian Corps was the first time all Canadian forces fought together as a unified national army rather than as British Army attachments. Its victories — especially at Vimy Ridge and in the Hundred Days Offensive of 1918 — established Canada as a leading military power and drove the demand for greater Canadian political sovereignty.
