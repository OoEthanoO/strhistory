---
term: Pax Mongolica
when: c. 1250s–1350s
definition: The "Mongol Peace" — the period when Mongol control of much of Eurasia made overland trade and travel relatively safe.
significance: It encouraged exchange of goods, technologies and ideas between China, the Islamic world and Europe — Marco Polo's travels belong to this period — but it may also have helped the Black Death spread.
