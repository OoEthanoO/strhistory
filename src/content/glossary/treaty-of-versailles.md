---
term: Treaty of Versailles
when: 1919
definition: The peace treaty with Germany after the First World War — it took territory, limited the army to 100,000 men, demilitarised the Rhineland and imposed reparations.
significance: German resentment of the treaty was a powerful force that Hitler exploited; breaking its terms one by one was the first stage of his foreign policy.
related: [war-guilt-clause]
---
