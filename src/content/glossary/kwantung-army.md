---
term: Kwantung Army
when: 1906–1945
definition: The Japanese army garrison stationed in the Kwantung Leased Territory and along the South Manchuria Railway, which grew into Japan's largest and most autonomous field army.
significance: Its officers staged the Mukden Incident in 1931 without government approval. The army's ability to act first and have Tokyo accept the result is key evidence that the military, not the cabinet, was driving Japanese expansion.
