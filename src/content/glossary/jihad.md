---
term: Jihad
when: 12th century (in this context)
definition: In Islam, "struggle" or "striving" in the path of God; in its military sense, war to defend or extend Muslim rule.
significance: The call for jihad against the Franks was revived by Zengi, Nur al-Din and Saladin, uniting Syria and Egypt and leading to the recapture of Jerusalem in 1187.
