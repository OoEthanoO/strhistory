---
term: Great Leap Forward
when: 1958–1962
definition: Mao's campaign to transform China into an industrial and communist society at speed, through people's communes and mass mobilisation.
significance: It caused the Great Chinese Famine and weakened Mao's position, leading to the recovery policies of Liu Shaoqi and Deng Xiaoping.
related: [peoples-communes, great-chinese-famine]
---
