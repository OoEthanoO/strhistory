---
term: Great Terror
when: 1936–1938
definition: The peak of Stalin's repression, including show trials of leading Bolsheviks, a purge of the Red Army, and mass arrests and executions across Soviet society.
significance: It eliminated any possible challenge to Stalin, but damaged the army and economy on the eve of war. It is central to debates about how far Stalin's rule depended on terror.
related: [show-trials, nkvd, gulag]
---
