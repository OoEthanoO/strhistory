---
term: Appeasement
when: 1930s
definition: The policy of avoiding war by making concessions to an aggressor's grievances, associated with Neville Chamberlain's dealings with Hitler.
significance: It reached its peak at Munich in 1938 and was abandoned after Germany occupied Prague in 1939. Historians still debate whether it was cowardice or a realistic policy for a weak Britain.
related: [munich-agreement]
---
