---
term: Long March
when: 1934–1935
definition: The retreat of the Communist Red Army from its base in Jiangxi to Yan'an in north-west China — some 9,000 km — to escape Guomindang encirclement.
significance: Only a fraction of the marchers survived, but it became the CCP's founding legend, and Mao emerged as the party's leading figure during it.
