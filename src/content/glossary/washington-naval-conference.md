---
term: Washington Naval Conference
when: 1921–1922
definition: A conference that limited the battleship fleets of the major naval powers — fixing a 5:5:3 ratio for the US, Britain and Japan — and guaranteed China's territorial integrity.
significance: Japanese nationalists resented the lower ratio as a humiliation. The collapse of this "Washington system" in the 1930s is central to arguments that Japan was reacting against an unequal international order.
