---
term: Salient
when: 1914–1918
definition: A military situation in which an enemy can attack from multiple sides against a position protruding into enemy lines.
significance: The Ypres Salient was one of the most dangerous positions on the Western Front — Allied forces inside it were surrounded on three sides by German lines. Holding it was costly, but abandoning it would have surrendered the vital supply route to Britain.
