---
term: Salient
when: 1914–1918
definition: A part of the front line that bulges into enemy territory, so that the enemy can attack it from several sides.
significance: "The Ypres Salient was one of the most dangerous positions on the Western Front: Allied forces inside it faced German lines on three sides. Holding it was costly, but giving it up would have opened the way towards the Channel ports through which British troops and supplies arrived."
