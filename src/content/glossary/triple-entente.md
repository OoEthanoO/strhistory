---
term: Triple Entente
when: 1907–1917
definition: The informal alignment of France, Russia and Britain, built from the Franco-Russian alliance (1894), the Entente Cordiale (1904) and the Anglo-Russian Entente (1907).
significance: Germany saw it as encirclement. Britain's commitments were looser than a formal alliance — which is why Belgium, not the Entente, decided its entry into the war.
related: [triple-alliance]
---
