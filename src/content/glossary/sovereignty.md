---
term: Sovereignty
when: 1867–1931
definition: A country’s authority to govern itself, both at home (domestic affairs) and in its dealings with other countries (foreign affairs).
significance: Canada's lack of sovereignty over foreign affairs meant it could not avoid the First World War. The sacrifices of 1914–1918 built political pressure to close this gap, making sovereignty a central issue of Canadian identity and constitutional development.
related: [dominion]
