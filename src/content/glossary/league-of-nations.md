---
term: League of Nations
when: 1920–1946
definition: The international organisation created by the Paris peace settlement to keep peace through collective security, arbitration and disarmament.
significance: Its failure to stop Japan in Manchuria (1931–33) and Italy in Abyssinia (1935–36) showed that collective security depended on great powers willing to impose sanctions or use force — which Britain and France were not, and the United States was not a member.
