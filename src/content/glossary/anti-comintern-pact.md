---
term: Anti-Comintern Pact
when: 25 November 1936
definition: An agreement between Germany and Japan to cooperate against the Communist International; Italy joined in 1937.
significance: It aligned the revisionist powers ideologically against the USSR and paved the way for the Tripartite Pact of 1940, linking the crises in Europe and Asia.
related: [tripartite-pact]
---
