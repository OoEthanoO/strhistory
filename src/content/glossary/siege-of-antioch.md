---
term: Siege of Antioch
when: October 1097 – June 1098
definition: The crusaders' eight-month siege of Antioch, followed by their own encirclement in the city by the army of Kerbogha of Mosul, whom they defeated on 28 June 1098.
significance: The turning point of the crusade. Starvation and desertion nearly destroyed the army, while victory — helped by Kerbogha's divided coalition — opened the road to Jerusalem.
