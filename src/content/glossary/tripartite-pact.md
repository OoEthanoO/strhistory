---
term: Tripartite Pact
when: 27 September 1940
definition: A defensive alliance between Germany, Italy and Japan, pledging mutual assistance if any of them was attacked by a power not already in the war — aimed chiefly at the United States.
significance: Intended to deter the US, it instead convinced American leaders that Japan was part of a single Axis threat, stiffening US opposition to Japanese expansion.
related: [anti-comintern-pact]
---
