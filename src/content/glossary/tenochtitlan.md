---
term: Tenochtitlan
when: c. 1325–1521
definition: The Mexica capital, built on an island in Lake Texcoco, with causeways, canals and a great temple precinct.
significance: One of the largest cities in the world in 1519. Its fall in August 1521 marked the collapse of Mexica power, and Mexico City was built on its ruins as the capital of New Spain.
