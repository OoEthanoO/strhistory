---
term: Nanjing Massacre
when: December 1937 – January 1938
definition: The mass killing of Chinese prisoners and civilians and widespread rape by Japanese troops after the capture of Nanjing, the Chinese Nationalist capital.
significance: It hardened Chinese resistance, damaged Japan's international reputation, and remains a source of tension between China and Japan. Estimates of the dead vary widely and are themselves part of the historical debate.
