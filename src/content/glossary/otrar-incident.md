---
term: Otrar incident
when: 1218
definition: The massacre of a Mongol trade caravan by the Khwarazmian governor of Otrar, followed by the shah's execution of a Mongol envoy sent to demand justice.
significance: It gave Genghis Khan his reason for invading Khwarazm — an example of how the Mongols treated attacks on merchants and envoys as grounds for war.
---
