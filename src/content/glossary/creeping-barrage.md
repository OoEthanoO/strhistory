---
term: Creeping Barrage
when: 1916–1918
definition: An artillery technique in which shells are fired in a moving curtain just ahead of advancing infantry, timed to lift forward at intervals as the troops advance.
significance: Perfected by Canadian artillerymen at Vimy Ridge (1917), the creeping barrage kept enemy defenders suppressed until Allied troops were almost upon them, overcoming the fatal gap between the end of preliminary bombardment and the infantry charge. It became standard Allied practice in the final offensives of 1918.
