---
term: Umkhonto we Sizwe (MK)
when: from 1961
definition: The armed wing ("Spear of the Nation") co-founded by Nelson Mandela, which began a sabotage campaign in December 1961.
significance: It marked the ANC's move from nonviolence to armed struggle; the capture of its leaders at Rivonia in 1963 crippled resistance inside South Africa.
