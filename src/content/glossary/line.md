---
term: Line
when: 1914–1918
definition: The continuous and unbroken positions which an army occupies against its enemy.
significance: Maintaining an unbroken front line was the primary strategic objective of trench warfare on the Western Front. Any rupture or gap in the line, such as the 6 km opening caused by the German gas attack at Ypres, threatened to collapse entire defensive salients and allow enemy encirclement unless immediately plugged.
