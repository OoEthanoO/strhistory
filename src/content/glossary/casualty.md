---
term: Casualty
when: 1914–1918
definition: A soldier lost to his unit because he has been killed, wounded, captured or gone missing.
significance: Casualty figures measure the human cost of war, counting the wounded and missing as well as the dead. The huge totals of First World War battles, such as about 6,000 Canadians at the Second Battle of Ypres or more than a million soldiers on both sides at the Somme, showed the attritional reality of industrial warfare.
