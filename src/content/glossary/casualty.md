---
term: Casualty
when: 1914–1918
definition: A soldier rendered incapable of fighting, either by death or injury.
significance: In military history, casualty rates measure the human cost of armed conflict and include both killed and wounded personnel. The staggering casualty totals of First World War battles—such as the 6,036 Canadians at Ypres or over 1.2 million Allied and German casualties at the Somme—demonstrated the attritional reality of modern industrial warfare.
