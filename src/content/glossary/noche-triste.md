---
term: Noche Triste
when: 30 June – 1 July 1520
definition: Spanish for "the sad night" — the Spaniards' disastrous night-time escape from Tenochtitlan, in which many were killed on the causeways.
significance: The Mexica had defeated the invaders. Cortés's survival depended on retreating to Tlaxcala, whose loyalty — and the smallpox epidemic that followed — allowed him to return.
