---
term: Kurultai
when: 1206 and after
definition: An assembly of Mongol princes and leaders that chose a new khan and decided major campaigns.
significance: The kurultai of 1206 gave Temüjin legitimacy as Genghis Khan over all the steppe peoples. Later kurultais to choose his successors could halt campaigns, as when the army withdrew from Europe after Ögedei's death in 1241.
