---
term: Cultural Revolution
when: 1966–1976
definition: The Great Proletarian Cultural Revolution, Mao's campaign against alleged "capitalist roaders" in the party and "old" culture.
significance: It removed Mao's rivals and strengthened his cult, but caused widespread violence, persecution, and damage to education and culture.
related: [red-guards, little-red-book, gang-of-four]
