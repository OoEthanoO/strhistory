---
term: Seljuk Turks
when: 11th–12th centuries
definition: A Turkic Sunni Muslim dynasty that conquered Persia, Iraq and much of Anatolia, defeating the Byzantines at Manzikert in 1071.
significance: Their advance prompted the Byzantine appeal for help that led to the First Crusade — and their fragmentation after 1092 helped the crusaders succeed.
