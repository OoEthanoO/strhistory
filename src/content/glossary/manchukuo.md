---
term: Manchukuo
when: 1932–1945
definition: The nominally independent state Japan created in Manchuria, headed by Puyi, the last Qing emperor, but controlled by the Kwantung Army and Japanese officials.
significance: A puppet state that let Japan claim it was supporting local self-determination while exploiting Manchuria's resources. Almost no country recognised it, which shows how isolated Japan's actions left it.
