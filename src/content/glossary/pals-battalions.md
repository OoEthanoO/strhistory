---
term: Pals Battalions
when: 1914–1916
definition: A British system of recruiting men from the same town, city or region into a single military unit, so that they could serve alongside their friends and neighbours.
significance: Pals Battalions boosted recruitment in 1914 but had a devastating effect on British communities at the Somme. When a battalion was wiped out, entire towns lost a generation of young men in a single day. The practice was largely discontinued after 1916. It did not occur in the Canadian Expeditionary Force.
