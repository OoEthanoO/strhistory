---
term: Pals Battalions
when: 1914–1916
definition: "British battalions of volunteers from the same town, city, workplace or district, who were promised that they could serve alongside friends and neighbours. Imagine a Markham Regiment made up of all the Grade 12 students from the city’s high schools: one disastrous attack at the Somme could kill or wound a whole generation of the city in minutes."
significance: Pals battalions boosted recruitment in 1914 and 1915 but had a devastating effect on British communities at the Somme. When a battalion was cut down, a town could lose many of its young men in a single day. After 1916, conscription and replacements from across Britain ended their local character. Canada also raised battalions locally, but most were broken up in England to reinforce units already at the front.
