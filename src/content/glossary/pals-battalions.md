---
term: Pals Battalions
when: 1914–1916
definition: A British practice of grouping men from a town, city or region in a single geographically linked unit. This did not occur in Canada, only in Britain. E.g a hypothetical Markham Regiment composed of all G12s from its high schools. If the regiment went to the Somme, the entire 09’ generation of a city could be killed in minutes.
significance: Pals Battalions boosted recruitment in 1914 but had a devastating effect on British communities at the Somme. When a battalion was wiped out, entire towns lost a generation of young men in a single day. The practice was largely discontinued after 1916. It did not occur in the Canadian Expeditionary Force.
