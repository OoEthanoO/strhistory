## Our course

Standard Level follows the revised History course first assessed in 2028. The department's choices are **Political and economic transitions** for Paper 1 and **Authoritarian rule** for Paper 2. These studies are shared with Higher Level.

The [notes library](/topics) organises individual events by date and place. The globe connects those events to a wider historical setting; each pin opens a separate note. The SL view includes the shared course, while the HL view adds the Europe studies.

## Learning through inquiry

Students practise close reading, source comparison and arguments supported by evidence. Short event notes offer starting points for revision and discussion; they do not yet cover every part of the course. Older notes remain accessible as an explicitly labelled archive.

Assessment information: [IB History subject brief, first assessment 2028](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/programmes/dp/pdfs/dp-history-sb-en.pdf). Detailed content requirements and examination instructions should be read alongside the teacher's current subject guide.
