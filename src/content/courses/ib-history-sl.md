Standard Level shares its core with Higher Level: a prescribed subject assessed through sources in Paper 1, and two world history topics assessed through essays in Paper 2. The internal assessment is a historical investigation on a question of your own choice.

*The units listed here are a sample. Update `src/content/courses/ib-history-sl.md` with the units your school teaches.*
