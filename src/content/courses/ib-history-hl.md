## Our course

Higher Level follows the revised History course first assessed in 2028 and includes all of the shared Standard Level content:

- **Paper 1:** Political and economic transitions.
- **Paper 2:** Authoritarian rule.
- **Paper 3:** Europe, with the department's selected studies of the French Revolution and the unification of Germany and Italy.

## From events to arguments

The HL notes view includes both shared notes and the Europe modules. Separate pins locate events such as the Tennis Court Oath, the proclamation of Italy and the proclamation of the German Empire. Combining their evidence supports comparisons of political authority, participation and national identity.

The library is being developed event by event and does not yet provide complete course coverage. Teacher profiles and pre-IB listings that remain samples are labelled separately.

Assessment information: [IB History subject brief, first assessment 2028](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/programmes/dp/pdfs/dp-history-sb-en.pdf). The public brief confirms the regional framework; the teacher's current subject guide supplies the detailed scope of each selected study.
