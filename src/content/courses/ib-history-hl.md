Higher Level students study the same core as Standard Level — one prescribed subject and two world history topics — and add three sections of a regional option for Paper 3. The course develops the skills historians use: evaluating sources, weighing interpretations, and building an argument supported by evidence.

## What you will study

- **Paper 1:** the move to global war, 1931–1941 — Japanese expansion in East Asia and German and Italian expansion.
- **Paper 2:** authoritarian states and the Cold War.
- **Paper 3:** three sections of the History of Europe option.

*The units listed here are a sample. Update `src/content/courses/ib-history-hl.md` with the units your school teaches.*

## Is Higher Level right for me?

Choose HL if you enjoy reading widely and writing extended arguments. HL students write five exam essays across Papers 2 and 3, so strong essay-writing habits matter from the first term.
