## What it is

The extended essay (EE) is an independent research essay of **up to 4,000 words**, part of the IB Diploma core. A history EE investigates a focused question about the past using primary and secondary sources, and makes a sustained argument.

## How it is assessed

The essay is marked out of 34 against five criteria: **focus and method**, **knowledge and understanding**, **critical thinking**, **presentation**, and **engagement**. Engagement is judged from your reflections on the planning and progress form, written during three reflection sessions with your supervisor.

## Choosing a topic

- Choose something you want to spend months on.
- Narrow it down in time, place and scale.
- Make sure there are sources — especially primary sources — you can reach.
- Avoid very recent events: a history EE needs enough distance for historical sources and interpretations to exist. Your supervisor will confirm what is acceptable.

## From topic to research question

A research question should be **focused, open to argument, and answerable in 4,000 words**.

| Too broad | Better |
| --- | --- |
| What caused the Cold War? | To what extent was the Berlin Blockade (1948–49) a response to the introduction of the Deutsche Mark? |
| Why did apartheid end? | How significant was the 1976 Soweto uprising in changing the ANC's strategy? |

Use the globe and notes on this site to explore periods and places that interest you — then talk to your supervisor early. Most strong essays go through several versions of their question.
